
ROLE:
Senior AI Engineer — Founding Team

COMPANY CONTEXT:
Redrob AI is an AI-native talent intelligence platform.
The role owns the intelligence layer of the product, including
ranking, retrieval, and candidate-job matching systems.

PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITY:
Build, improve, and own production ranking, retrieval, and matching
systems that determine what recruiters see when searching for
candidates and what candidates see when searching for roles.

The role requires both:
- Deep technical capability in modern ML systems.
- A practical product-engineering mindset focused on shipping,
  testing with real users, and improving systems based on evidence.

EXPERIENCE:
- Stated experience range: 5-9 years.
- Candidates outside this range may still be considered when other
  signals are strong.
- Ideal profile: approximately 6-8 years of total experience,
  including 4-5 years in applied ML/AI roles at product companies.

CRITICAL REQUIREMENTS:
1. Production experience with embeddings-based retrieval systems,
   such as sentence-transformers, OpenAI embeddings, BGE, E5, or similar.
2. Experience deploying retrieval systems to real users, including
   practical experience with embedding drift, index refresh, and
   retrieval-quality regression.
3. Production experience with vector databases or hybrid-search
   infrastructure, such as Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant, Milvus,
   OpenSearch, Elasticsearch, FAISS, or similar.
4. Strong Python and production coding experience.
5. Hands-on experience designing evaluation frameworks for ranking
   systems, including NDCG, MRR, MAP, offline-to-online correlation,
   and A/B test interpretation.
6. Experience shipping an end-to-end ranking, search, or recommendation
   system to real users at meaningful scale is an important signal.

PREFERRED REQUIREMENTS:
- LLM fine-tuning using LoRA, QLoRA, or PEFT.
- Learning-to-rank models, including XGBoost-based or neural approaches.
- HR-tech, recruiting-tech, or marketplace product experience.
- Distributed systems or large-scale inference optimization.
- Open-source contributions in AI/ML.

IMPORTANT TECHNICAL SIGNALS:
- Strong understanding of retrieval, including hybrid and dense retrieval.
- Practical experience with ranking systems and ranking evaluation.
- Experience integrating LLMs into production systems.
- Ability to make practical decisions about fine-tuning versus prompting.
- Production ML/AI experience rather than only theoretical or academic work.
- Recent hands-on production coding experience.

PROFILE CONSIDERATIONS:
The JD explicitly states that pure research experience without
production deployment is not the target profile.

The JD also states that recent AI experience consisting primarily
of simple LangChain projects calling OpenAI, without substantial
pre-LLM ML production experience, is not the target.

The role requires engineers who continue to write production code;
a candidate whose recent experience is only architecture or
technical leadership without recent production coding is not the
target profile.

A background primarily in computer vision, speech, or robotics
without significant NLP/information-retrieval exposure is also
not the target profile.

PRODUCT-COMPANY SIGNAL:
The ideal profile includes applied ML/AI experience at product
companies. The JD specifically distinguishes product-company
experience from candidates whose entire career has been at
consulting/service companies.

IDEAL CANDIDATE:
Approximately 6-8 years of total experience, including 4-5 years
in applied ML/AI roles at product companies, with experience
shipping at least one end-to-end ranking, search, or recommendation
system to real users at meaningful scale.

The ideal candidate demonstrates strong practical knowledge of
retrieval, ranking evaluation, and LLM integration, and can explain
technical decisions using evidence from systems they have actually built.
